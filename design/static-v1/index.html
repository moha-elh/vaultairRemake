07 // Questions

Asked, answered.

How Vaultaire compares
vs Apple Hidden Album vs Keepsafe vs Private Photo Vault vs Calculator apps
What is Vaultaire?

A local-first encrypted vault for iPhone and iPad. Files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a random master key, unlocked by a pattern you draw. No account.

What if I forget my pattern?

Use your nine-word recovery phrase to regain access. Without the pattern or the phrase, nobody — including us — can decrypt your vault.

Can Apple see my files?

No. Everything is encrypted on your device before iCloud receives it. Apple only ever stores ciphertext.

Do I need an account?

No account, no email, no phone number. Install it and draw a pattern.

Do you optimize my data?

Photos and videos are stored in an optimized form by default. Switch to “Original” in Settings to keep full quality.

How does sharing work?

Share a vault with a secret phrase. You can limit opens, set an expiry and block export. Creating a share needs Pro; opening one is free.

Do you support folders?

Yes — folders and nested folders, with custom ordering.

What can I store?

Photos, videos, documents, notes — any file type.

How many vaults can I have?

Up to 3 on Free. Unlimited on Pro.

How many files per vault?

Free includes 100 photos and 10 videos in total. Pro is unlimited.

Do you collect analytics?

Anonymous usage and crash data, on by default. Turn it off any time with “Share errors anonymously” in Settings.
