04 // How it works

From pattern to ciphertext in three moves.

I.
Draw your pattern

At least six dots and one change of direction on a 5×5 grid. That’s your key — nothing to type, nothing to remember on paper.

PBKDF2 · DEVICE-WIDE KEYCHAIN SALT
II.
Load the vault

Import photos, videos, documents and notes — or shoot straight into it with the secure camera.

AES-256-GCM · ENCRYPTED ON ENTRY
III.
Lock & vanish

Locking discards the active key state. What’s left on the device is ciphertext — and an unlock screen that lists nothing.
